## 📊 Project Overview

attrition and understand how factors such as department, job role,
overtime, job satisfaction, work-life balance, salary, and experience
relate to employee turnover.

## 🔍 Key Insights

-   **1,470 employees** were included in the dataset.
-   **237 employees left**, resulting in an overall attrition rate of
    **16.12%**.
-   **Sales Representatives** had the highest job-role attrition rate at
    approximately **39.8%**.
-   Employees working overtime had a higher attrition rate
    (**approximately 30.5%**) than employees not working overtime
    (**approximately 10.4%**).
-   Low job satisfaction was associated with higher attrition:
    approximately **22.8%** at satisfaction level 1 versus **11.3%** at
    level 4.
-   The lowest work-life balance level had an attrition rate of
    approximately **31.2%**.
-   The Research & Development department had the largest workforce.

*These results show associations in the dataset; they do not establish
that any single factor causes employees to leave.*
